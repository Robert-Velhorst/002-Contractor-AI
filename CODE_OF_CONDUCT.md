# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment
We want everyone who participates in this project to have a respectful, safe, and constructive experience.

## Expected behavior
- Be considerate, patient, and respectful.
- Discuss ideas and work, not personal traits.
- Accept constructive feedback and differing viewpoints.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's private information.

## Unacceptable behavior
Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate intimidation, personal attacks, doxxing, sexualized attention, and other conduct that a reasonable person would find harmful are not acceptable.

## Enforcement
Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when this Code is violated. They will consider the nature and impact of the conduct.

## Reporting
To report a conduct concern privately, email [noodzakelijkonline@gmail.com](mailto:noodzakelijkonline@gmail.com). Avoid putting personal or sensitive details in a public issue. You may also use GitHub's [report abuse guidance](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) for the relevant profile, repository, issue, pull request, or comment. For security vulnerabilities, use this repository's private vulnerability reporting feature when available.
